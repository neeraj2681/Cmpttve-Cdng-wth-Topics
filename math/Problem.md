You are given a chemical equation of the following format:<br>
(b1)C<sub>x</sub> + (b2)H<sub>y</sub> --------> (b3)C<sub>p</sub>H<sub>q</sub><br><br>

Your task is to calculate the balancing factor b1, b2, and b3 such that the number of atoms of each of the elements is equal on both sides.<br>

***Note: The answer must be in a reduced form.***<br>

***Input format***<br>
The first line consists of four integers x, y, p, and q.<br><br>

***Output format***<br>
The output must contain b1, b2, and b3.<br><br>

***Constraints***<br>
<code>1<=x<=10000<br>
1<=y<=10000<br>
1<=p<=10000<br>
1<=q<=10000<br></code><br>

***Sample Input***<br>
<code>2 3 4 5</code><br><br>

***Sample Output***<br>
<code>6 5 3</code><br><br>

***Explanation***<br>

Given chemical equation is :<br>

C<sub>2</sub>  +  H<sub>3</sub>     -------->    C<sub>4</sub> H<sub>5</sub><br>

The balanced chemical equation is:<br>

6 C<sub>2</sub>  + 5 H<sub>3</sub>     -------->    3 C<sub>4</sub> H<sub>5</sub>
